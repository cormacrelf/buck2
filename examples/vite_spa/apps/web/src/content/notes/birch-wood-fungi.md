A wet fortnight and then two mild days, which is usually the signal.

## What was up

The fly agarics were the obvious ones, a ring of eleven under the big
birch by the gate, some still closed like eggs and some fully open and
starting to fade at the edges. Further in, along the mossy bank by the
old wall, a small group of chanterelles. Left them where they were.

## Smaller things

| Where              | What                       | How many |
| ------------------ | -------------------------- | -------: |
| Fallen birch trunk | Birch polypore (brackets)  |        6 |
| Leaf litter        | Small brown things, unknown |      lots |
| Gatepost           | Robin, following me round  |        1 |

The robin stayed within a few metres the whole time, picking at whatever I
turned over.
