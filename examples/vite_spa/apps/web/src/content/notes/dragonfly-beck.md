Hot and still. Sat by the pool below the footbridge for most of the
afternoon.

## Golden-ringed

At least two golden-ringed dragonflies patrolling the same stretch, each
flying a low beat up and down the water and clashing whenever they met in
the middle. A female came in once and dipped her abdomen into the gravel at
the edge, over and over.

## And then

On the walk out, fresh spraint on the flat rock under the bridge, still
wet. No sign of the otter itself, but it's the first evidence on this beck
since I started keeping notes.
