Visibility about fifty metres all day. Navigated by the old boundary
stones, which are about that far apart, so it worked out.

## Sound

With nothing to look at you hear more. Water running under the heather in
places where there's no visible stream. A single pipit somewhere off to the
left, and a long way off what might have been a curlew, the first of the
year if it was.

## Notes on the route

1. From the cattle grid, follow the wall north to its corner.
2. Strike off on a bearing of `035°` to the first stone.
3. Stone to stone after that; there are nine.
4. The last one is cracked and leans east.

Heather still brown and wiry. Nothing in flower.
