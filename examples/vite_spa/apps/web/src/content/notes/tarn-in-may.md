Left the car at the bottom of the lane just after eight. The beck was loud
from three days of rain and the path up through the intake wall was more
stream than path.

## The outflow

A dipper was working the stones where the tarn spills out, bobbing on each
rock before it walked straight under the water. Watched it for ten minutes;
it came up with something every second or third dive.

## The bog on the north shore

The sundews are out early. Counted about forty rosettes in the wet moss
between the two boulders, most of them with a midge or two stuck to the
leaves. Meadow pipits everywhere on the slope above, parachuting down
mid-song.

> Note for next time: the stepping stones on the far side are under water
> after rain. Go round by the wall.

Back down by eleven, before the cloud came in.
